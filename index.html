About

Jérémie Urbain is a filmmaker from Paris and New York City, currently based in Montréal. Urbain's works blend experimental filmmaking with a narrative lens. Letting the imagery allure the audience is key throughout his pieces. Urbain's practice, often opting for beauty over narrative, relies heavily on editing, whether it be layering, colour grading or continuity. Urbain holds a bachelor's degree in Film Production from the Mel Hoppenheim School of Cinema.
